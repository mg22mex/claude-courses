# Weatherman AI Portal — Flight Manual

Welcome to the Weatherman AI Portal. This is your one-page guide to getting started. There are exactly three things you need to know.

---

## Step 1 — Log Into the Portal

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the Weatherman AI Portal URL (provided by your administrator).
2. You will see a clean login screen. Enter the credentials you received during onboarding.
3. Once authenticated, you will land on the portal dashboard — this is your home base for all exercises.

> **Tip:** Bookmark the URL. You will use this same link every time.

---

## Step 2 — Select Your Profile

1. Look for the **sidebar** on the left side of the screen.
2. Click the dropdown menu at the top of the sidebar — it may be labeled "Workspace" or "Profile."
3. Select **your name** from the list:
   - **Rick** — Executive, Entrepreneurship & Management
   - **Sunny** — Purchasing & Logistics
   - **Mollie** — Sales & Financials
   - **Christine** — Marketing
   - **Paula & Gaby** — Creative Design
4. The portal will automatically load your pre-configured API settings and department system prompt. You are now ready to work.

> **Tip:** If you need to switch to a different department's preset mid-session, just return to the sidebar dropdown and select a different profile. The portal will reconfigure itself.

---

## Step 3 — Upload Your Files and Start Prompting

1. Look for the **paperclip icon** (📎) or **Upload** button near the chat input area at the bottom of the screen.
2. Click it and select the business file you want to work with from your computer. Supported formats:
   - **Spreadsheets:** `.csv`, `.xlsx`
   - **Documents:** `.pdf`, `.md`, `.txt`, `.docx`
   - **Images:** `.png`, `.jpg`, `.svg`
3. Once the file is attached, type your prompt into the chat input box using natural language. For example:
   - *"Analyze this vendor CSV and show me which suppliers have the longest lead times."*
   - *"Review this ad copy deck for brand compliance issues."*
   - *"Generate a profit margin report from this sales data."*
4. Press **Enter** or click the **Send** button. The portal AI will process your request and return the results in the chat window.
5. You can download any generated report or document using the **Download** button that appears alongside responses.

> **Tip:** Be specific in your prompts. The more context you provide about what you need, the better the portal AI can tailor its response to your business situation.

---

## Support

If you encounter any issues:
- **Login problems** — Contact your administrator to verify your credentials.
- **Missing profile** — Ensure your administrator has set up your workspace with the correct department preset.
- **File upload errors** — Check that your file is in one of the supported formats listed above.
